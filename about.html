About

Built for the hard problems.

I'm Kyle Leighton, a Senior SDET based in Huntington Beach, CA with 10+ years of experience building mobile test automation infrastructure for some of the most demanding software environments in the world - FDA-regulated medical devices and high-scale consumer applications.

I spent nine years testing software where failures had direct consequences for patients - at Medtronic, Boston Scientific, and Embecta building automation frameworks for Class II and III medical devices. Today I build automation for Chipotle's consumer mobile application, used by millions of people every day.

What makes me different is the combination: deep native mobile expertise across all three major automation stacks (Appium, Espresso, XCUITest), hands-on AI tooling experience with Claude and Claude Code, and the engineering discipline that comes from building quality systems in regulated environments.

I work with engineering teams who need a mobile QA specialist who can hit the ground running - no ramp-up, no hand-holding, just results.
